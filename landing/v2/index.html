Agents

Call an agent the way you’d call a teammate.

Type @ in any chat. Researcher, Analyst or an agent you made picks up the job, shows its steps and hands back a page, a sheet or a list of tasks. Everyone in the chat sees what it did.

  • Agents split big jobs into subagents and work in parallel.
  • Turn any good answer into a reusable agent with one click.
  • An agent only sees what the person who called it can see.

Bots

Bots that keep working after you log off.

Talk to a bot, change it by talking, give it a schedule. It sends the Monday digest, checks churn every evening and waits for a person before anything leaves the team.

  • Every bot has three tabs: Talk, Edit, Schedule.
  • “Every Friday at 4, remind the team to close tasks” becomes a schedule.
  • You choose what it can touch and what it must ask about first.
